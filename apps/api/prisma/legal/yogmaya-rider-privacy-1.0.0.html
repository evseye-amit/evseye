Rider Privacy Policy

EVsEye Rider App | Fleet Operator: Yogmaya
Effective date: 01 October 2026 | Version: 1.0.0

This Policy explains how personal information is used when a Rider uses the EVsEye Rider App for Yogmaya. It applies to the features made available to that Rider. If a feature is not enabled, the information needed only for that feature is not requested through it.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Yogmaya, Gurugram, decides how Rider information is used for onboarding, identity and eligibility checks, vehicle allocation, payments, and fleet operations. EV Spares India Pvt Ltd independently decides how information is used for EVsEye account security, platform support, and service analytics. EV Spares also operates the EVsEye platform on Yogmaya's behalf for Yogmaya's Rider operations. Contact details for both organisations are in section 14.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the enabled service, information may include your name, mobile number, date of birth, address, photograph, emergency or reference contacts, driving licence, onboarding answers, vehicle allocation and usage, payment and wallet transactions, support requests, training completion, and referral records. Identity checks may use PAN, Aadhaar-related verification information, and bank-account verification information where requested in the App. The specific fields required for a feature are shown when you provide them.

3. Identity verification and Aadhaar

Where an identity check is required, verification information is shared with Sandbox, our KYC service provider, for the requested check. The platform stores verification results and masked identifiers needed to administer the check; identity numbers are not displayed publicly. Aadhaar verification, if enabled, is used only for the disclosed identity-check purpose. Riders may ask what verification information is held about them using the contact details below.

4. Location, vehicle IoT, and app permissions

If you choose to use nearby-hub features and grant location permission, the App uses your phone's location while the App is in use to find relevant hubs. The App does not collect phone location in the background for Yogmaya Riders. A vehicle with GPS or IoT hardware can independently transmit its location, battery, speed, ignition, and other vehicle-status or safety events. That hardware can operate when the App is closed and may continue operating outside an active shift while powered. Vehicle IoT information is used for fleet operations, safety, maintenance, and theft or incident investigation.

5. Face verification

Face matching and liveness verification are not active for Yogmaya Riders. If either is introduced, we will provide a separate explanation of the images and results collected, the provider, retention, and available choices before use.

6. Why we use information

Yogmaya uses Rider information to create and manage Rider accounts, verify identity and eligibility, allocate and administer vehicles, manage rent, deposits, invoices and payments, provide training and support, maintain vehicle safety, investigate incidents and suspected fraud, and meet applicable legal obligations. EV Spares uses information to secure accounts and the EVsEye service, respond to platform support requests, troubleshoot and improve service reliability, and analyse service use. Referral information is used only where a referral feature is enabled.

7. Notice, permissions, and choices

We present the information requested for each feature when it is collected. Where consent is required, it is requested for the stated purpose and may be withdrawn as applicable under law by contacting us. Optional phone-location permission can be denied or changed in your device settings; nearby-hub results may then be unavailable or less relevant. Withdrawal of information necessary to provide a requested Rider service may limit that service, subject to legal and contractual obligations.

8. Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only as needed for the purposes above with authorised Yogmaya personnel and EV Spares platform personnel, and with service providers under appropriate access controls. Current or applicable providers include Sandbox for enabled KYC checks, Razorpay and Cashfree for payment processing, MSG91 for email delivery, and Amazon Web Services for hosting. Where a feature is enabled, an SMS provider may process a phone number and one-time code, and a map or routing service may process a location used for a route or nearby-hub search. A provider receives only the information needed for its task. Information may also be disclosed to insurers, maintenance partners, or authorities where needed for a service, claim, safety matter, or legal requirement.

9. Client separation and access

The platform separates Yogmaya's operational data from other fleet operators' data. Access is limited by user role and the operational task. EV Spares staff may access information for approved support, security, maintenance, or legal purposes; access is not represented as zero. Requests for emergency or legally compelled access are handled according to the applicable process.

10. Storage, retention, and security

Core platform information is hosted on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in India. The exact AWS region can vary by deployment. A service provider's processing location may differ; information about a specific provider can be requested using the contacts below. Access controls, audit records, and technical safeguards are used to protect information according to the service configuration.

KYC and identity-check records, vehicle GPS/IoT history, and support records are retained for up to one year from collection, unless a longer period is necessary for an active legal hold, investigation, dispute, or applicable law. Payment, invoice, tax, and accounting records are retained for the period required by applicable law; where GST record-keeping rules apply, this is generally at least 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the relevant year, and longer where proceedings require it. Account and active vehicle-allocation information is retained while needed to provide and administer the Rider service, then reviewed for deletion or de-identification subject to legal and dispute requirements. Retention and deletion are administered by the responsible teams.

11. Your rights and grievances

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, correction or updating of inaccurate information, erasure where appropriate, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and resolution of a grievance. Send a request to the privacy contact in section 14. We may verify your identity before acting and may retain information when a legal obligation, live dispute, or other lawful ground requires it. Any applicable nomination right may be exercised through the same contact.

12. Age and eligibility

The Rider service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Yogmaya does not knowingly onboard a minor as a vehicle Rider. If information about a minor is submitted in error, contact us so it can be reviewed and corrected or deleted as appropriate.

13. Security incidents and policy changes

The responsible organisation will assess suspected security incidents and provide notices when required by applicable law. Material changes to this Policy will be communicated through the App or another appropriate channel. The current version and effective date will be shown with the Policy; renewed consent will be sought when required.
