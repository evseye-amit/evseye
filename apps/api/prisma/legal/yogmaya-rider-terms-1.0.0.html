“Platform” means the EVsEye Rider App and supporting technology operated by Yogmaya. “Fleet Operator” means Yogmaya, the entity responsible for the vehicle and rider-facing rental/service arrangement. “Rider” means the individual using the App. Identify the actual contracting entities and their roles before publishing.

2.Eligibility and onboarding

Riders must be at least 18 years old, legally eligible to use the assigned vehicle, and hold a valid licence whenever required. Riders must provide accurate mobile, identity, address, licence and other onboarding details and update changes promptly. Approval and vehicle allocation may depend on verification and operator policies.

3.Account access and security

Access may use a registered mobile number and OTP. Riders must safeguard their devices and report suspected unauthorized access. Account access is personal and must not be sold or transferred.

4.Services and operator relationship

The App supports onboarding, verification, vehicle allocation, hubs, rentals, wallets, payments, maintenance reporting, referrals, training and support where enabled. 5.Vehicle use and safety

Follow traffic laws, safety requirements, vehicle capacity limits, manufacturer guidance and Fleet Operator instructions. Do not lend, sublet, tamper with, modify or use a vehicle or battery for unauthorized purposes. Report accidents, theft, damage, battery issues and breakdowns promptly.

Applicable onboarding fees, rentals, penalties, taxes, refundable deposits, deductions and billing cycles must be shown before acceptance or transaction. Location, vehicle age/category and plan may affect prices only where disclosed in the applicable rate card. A deposit is not automatically forfeited and any deductions must be itemized under the signed rental agreement.

7.Wallet, payments and mandates

Wallet balances, credits, refunds, failed payments and adjustments are displayed in the App where offered. Payment gateways and mandate providers may process payments. UPI AutoPay or eNACH is optional unless clearly disclosed as a condition of a particular rental product; separate, revocable mandate authorization and provider rules apply. No debit beyond a valid authorization is permitted.

Where vehicles are GPS/IoT enabled, trip, vehicle status, location and safety events may be processed as described in the Privacy Policy. Device or app location permissions must be explained in context. Do not claim tracking only occurs during shifts unless the deployed hardware and operations actually enforce this.
