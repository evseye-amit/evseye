# Rider Rate Card Phase 4: deposits and commercial subledger

## Source and lifecycle

Deposit obligations are initialized from `RiderRentalAgreement.pricingSnapshot.deposits`. The initializer verifies the agreement pricing hash, preserves the original, adjusted, waived, and final requirement in each snapshot line, and uses `index:ruleId:code` as the stable obligation key. It is idempotent. The Phase 3 acceptance transaction initializes the obligations before committing the new agreement. Rate card deposit masters are never consulted during initialization.

`RiderDeposit` retains searchable agreement, rider, vehicle, type, currency, requirement, and materialized balance fields. Rider security has no vehicle link; other deposits keep the accepted vehicle link. `RiderDepositTransaction` is append only. `WAIVER` records the accepted concession with zero cash and zero ledger effect. Collection, deduction, refund completion, transfer, adjustment, forfeiture, and reversal create positive-amount transactions with signed available and funding deltas, before/after balance, actor, reason, reference, and idempotency key. A migration trigger prevents updating or deleting transactions. Another trigger prevents changes to the accepted obligation and verifies that materialized balances equal the transaction sums on update.

Available balance is the sum of transaction `balanceDelta`. Funded amount is the sum of `fundingDelta`. Outstanding initial funding is `max(required - funded, 0)`; top-up required after deductions is `max(required - available, 0)`. Waivers never increase available cash. Reconciliation recalculates both materialized balances and fails on a mismatch. Balances cannot be negative.

All balance changes, transaction inserts, ledger inserts, and audit records share a serializable transaction. The deposit row is locked before movement. Transfers lock both deposits in sorted ID order, then post paired `TRANSFER_OUT` and `TRANSFER_IN` records with a shared transfer reference. Reversal is a full compensating transaction linked to its original; transfer and waiver transactions are not individually reversible. Refunds have a separate request record. The `REFUND` movement occurs only when operations records a confirmed payout reference.

## Ledger semantics

The existing `RiderLedgerEntry` is reused with a new `category`. Existing billing and payment entries default to `CHARGE_BALANCE`, where debits add charges and credits reduce them. Deposit movements use `REFUNDABLE_DEPOSIT_LIABILITY`: credits increase cash held for the rider; debits reduce it. They are excluded from the charge balance and are not rental revenue. A deposit ledger entry references its exact `RiderDepositTransaction` ID. This is a commercial subledger, not a full general ledger. Deposit deductions do not silently clear invoices or create revenue; a future authorized settlement can explicitly connect those domains.

## Activation and queries

Deposit readiness is one prerequisite of Phase 3 agreement activation; active allocation remains another. A client administrator may set `FULL_REQUIRED` (default), `MINIMUM_PERCENTAGE`, `MINIMUM_AMOUNT`, `WAIVER_ALLOWED`, or `NOT_REQUIRED_BEFORE_ACTIVATION`. Thresholds are required for minimum policies. The policy evaluates currently held cash less pending refund requests, so a preactivation deduction or refund request cannot satisfy the requirement merely because cash was collected earlier. Readiness does not auto-activate an agreement. Agreement and rider summaries keep outstanding charges, held refundable deposits, pending deposit requirement, and pending refunds separate. Settlement is a read-only calculation of available refund and close readiness; it does not invent deductions. A deposit may be explicitly closed after the agreement ends and its available balance and pending refunds reach zero.

Operations endpoints under `/api/v1/rider-deposits` cover view, history, collection, deduction, forfeiture, adjustment, refund request/completion, transfer, reversal, and reconciliation. `/api/v1/rider-rental-agreements/:id/deposits` covers initialization, summary, readiness, and settlement. `/api/v1/rider-deposit-policy` sets the client activation policy. Rider app deposit endpoints resolve the authenticated rider and return a limited display view. All reads and writes use authenticated client scope. Financial actions require an `Idempotency-Key`; deduction, refund, transfer, forfeiture, adjustment, and reversal require client administrator role.

## Scope

This phase records confirmed collections and payouts without a payment provider. It does not collect money through Cashfree, issue invoices, charge rent, run exchange workflows, or automatically offset rent with security deposits. The Phase 1–4 rate-card SQL applied successfully to a temporary PostgreSQL 15 database; the unrelated nearest-hub migration could not apply there because local PostGIS is unavailable. Live database checks exercised concurrent collection and deduction, refund retry, transfer pairing and rollback, reconciliation, and immutable financial records. The temporary database is not a production migration target.
